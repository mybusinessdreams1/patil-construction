04 / Before you start

Common questions.

Do you take on individual repair jobs?

Yes. Enquiries can cover a specific repair such as plumbing, roof repair or waterproofing, or a larger renovation. Share photos and the locality so we can discuss scope.

What should I send to get a quote?

Send the project locality, property type, what you want repaired or changed, and approximate measurements or photos if available. The final scope may need a site visit.

Do you handle kitchen and bathroom work?

Yes. We handle kitchen remodelling, plumbing and tile work. Tell us which fixtures and finishes you want to keep or replace.

Can I ask about structural repairs?

Yes. Share the issue and the property details. Structural concerns need a site assessment to determine the appropriate scope and professional input.
